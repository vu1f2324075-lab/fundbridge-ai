I. FINAL PROTOTYPE OBJECTIVE
============================================================

When a project guide uses the prototype, they should be able
to understand the entire concept without anyone explaining
every screen.

The prototype must answer these questions visually:

FOR STARTUPS:

1. What information do I provide?
2. How does FundBridge analyse my business?
3. How ready am I for funding?
4. Which investors may be relevant?
5. Why was an investor recommended?
6. Which government schemes may be relevant?
7. Why am I potentially eligible?
8. What is wrong with my proposal?
9. How can I improve it?
10. What should I do next?

FOR INVESTORS:

1. What investment preferences do I have?
2. Which businesses match my preferences?
3. Why was this business recommended?
4. What is the business asking for?
5. How strong is its profile?
6. What should I review?
7. Can I shortlist the business?
8. Can I review the proposal?

The final prototype must demonstrate both sides clearly.
